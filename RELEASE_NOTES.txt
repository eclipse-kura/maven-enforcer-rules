Eclipse Kura Maven Enforcer Rules - 1.0.0 - February 2026
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
The first version of the maven-enforcer-rules allows to apply additional rulesets to the org.apache.maven.plugins:maven-enforcer-plugin. This version provides the rule bannedPluginsAdvanced which extends the built-in rule bannedPlugins to specify also the goal to ban.
